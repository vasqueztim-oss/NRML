# Where we stand

NRML is clean news for everyday people: the facts that affect your life, without the team colors.

**Why we exist.** Most news tells you how to feel before it tells you what happened. Most Americans have more in common than they're told, and they're busy just getting through the day. NRML is an injection of normal into a world of us versus them.

**What we cover most.** Affordability, housing, and healthcare. If it touches what you pay, where you live, or how you get care, it's our beat.

**Our point of view.** We come from a progressive place, and we say so up front. When a progressive policy could fix a real problem, we explore it: where it's been tried, what it cost, and what happened. The reporting always comes first, and it's always labeled separately from our take.

**Policy before labels.** We care whether an idea lowers your rent or your bus fare, not which team it's tied to. We lead with the problem and the results.

**Hot-button issues.** Facts first, and only when they genuinely affect people's lives. When we share a view, it's open, not absolute: supporting stricter gun laws and respecting the Second Amendment aren't opposites. When our view runs against the grain, the other side gets a clear, fair hearing.

**What we won't do.** Chase rage bait or the viral fight of the day. Mock people who disagree. Tell you to hate your neighbor.

**What we hope you feel.** Informed about what's affecting your life, and convinced that paying attention and showing up is in your best interest, because it is.

**How we're made.** NRML is produced by an AI newsroom with strict rules: every fact links to its source, every story is fact-checked, and corrections are public. Our columnist, Norm, is an AI too, and Norm will never pretend otherwise.
